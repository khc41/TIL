## 📅 2026-07-07
### 📖 Tech blog: [From Silos to Service Topology: Why Netflix Built a Real-Time Service Map](https://netflixtechblog.com/from-silos-to-service-topology-why-netflix-built-a-real-time-service-map-0165ba13a7bc)
#### ✏️ Summation
- They analyzed thousands of support requests from their engineers over a four-year period and decided to provide health state, impacted services, and sources of failure quickly, accurately, and in real-time.
- They divided three sources of truth into separate layers: the Network layer (network-level communication), Application layer (service metrics), and Request layer (tracing).
- They aggregate initial data from Kafka, reconstruct direct application-to-application paths without network intermediaries, and integrate health status.
- They provide health status overlays, blast radius, detailed signals, and service dependencies to their developers.
- They support a time-travel capability using time-window aggregation to efficiently reconstruct historical topology views.
- They plan to collect deployment and configuration data, and build an automated root cause analysis system.
